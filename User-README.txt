ChronoStretch: quick start

1. Add ChronoStretch to a track. It opens empty: go to BROWSE, pick a sample and load it; it is chopped on its hits.
2. Play pads 1-16. Pad 1 is the first chop in the sound, pad 16 the last; the pads on screen sit like the MPC's
   own (pad 1 bottom left), each in its own colour. Under the waveform each chop has a tab in that colour with its
   number, and a line from the tab's point up through the waveform marks where the chop starts.
   Up to 64 chops, numbered 1-64 through banks A-D: set BANK (top right) and the same pads play that bank (17-32 on B,
   33-48 on C, 49-64 on D). CUES sets how many (16, 32, 48 or 64; 64 to start).
3. The chops play in time with your project (SYNC ON follows MPC's tempo) and in the key you pick. KEY - and + on
   the main page move the sound a semitone at a time; the box between them shows the sound's key and the key it now
   plays in (C MIN > D MIN). The tempo stays the same. The line above the waveform shows the tempo and key.
   1/2 SPEED (main page) plays the sound at half your project's tempo, pitch kept: program double-time drums over it.
4. CHOP MODE picks how the sound is chopped; the knob beside it changes with the mode:
   TRANSIENT  chops on the sound's hits. SENSITIVITY: turn it up for more chops, down for only the biggest hits.
   EQUAL      16, 32, 48 or 64 even slices (CUES).
   BPM        a chop every half beat, beat, 2 beats, bar or 2 bars on the sound's beat grid (CHOP EVERY).
   MANUAL     chop it yourself, like MPC's: choose MANUAL, hit any pad and the sound plays from the
              start; hit a pad at each place you want a chop (pad 2, 3, 4 ... in order). Choose another mode to finish early.
   Changing the mode or its knob chops again; the line under the waveform says how many chops it made.
   RANDOM (beside CHOP MODE) chops all four banks at places picked at random through the sound (its hits first); press it
   again for a new set, UNDO for the last one.
   ADD (under KEY) puts a chop where the sound is playing, or in the middle of the view; DEL removes the
   selected chop. UNDO (there too, and on the PAD and FX pages) takes back the last edit, up to 10 steps: chops,
   moves, pad settings, FX. STOP (bottom left, and on the PAD, FX and BROWSE pages) stops everything that plays
   (CONTINUE, HOLD, previews). SYNC and 1/2 SPEED sit top right; CUE, NUDGE (the selected pad's chop), PITCH and VOLUME
   in the knob row beside KEY.
5. Moving a chop: put your finger on its coloured tab and drag left or right. Double-tap a tab to select its chop
   (its pad), so CUE, NUDGE and the Q-Links work on it. ZOOM + and - close in
   on the selected chop a little at a time (1.09x a press; the ZOOM Q-Link turns it smoothly),
   down to a single sample a column; SCROLL moves the zoomed view. Drags move the chop smoothly
   to the sample; DRAG SPEED (SOUND page, FINEST to FASTEST) sets how far. NUDGE (main and PAD pages): one Q-Link click is
   one waveform column (10 ms at most; zoom in for finer), and the line under
   the waveform (and on the PAD page) says how far it moved. SNAP ZERO CROSS (SOUND page) puts every moved or added chop on the nearest
   zero crossing, for a clean loop start. Every chop also fades in over at least 1 ms, so none starts with a click.
6. PLAY MODE: ONE SHOT plays a pad's chop; CONTINUE keeps playing past it until you hit a pad again, or STOP.
7. DRUMS: OFF takes the drums out, SOLO keeps just them (ready once the screen says DRUMS READY).
8. KEY: the sound's key is found once its drums are separated; a drum loop has none (NO KEY). If it is wrong or
   missing, set SOUND KEY on the SOUND page. MOVE TO KEY there picks a key outright.
   A sample's name is read too: "85 BPM" in it sets the tempo, "C# min" or "Am" the key.
   TEMPO: the sound's BPM is found when it loads. If it drifts against your project, trim it with BPM FINE
   (SOUND page, +-1 BPM in hundredths), or set SOUND BPM outright; AUTO uses the one found.
9. The PAD page has everything about the selected pad (length, filter, envelope, speed, mode, choke group), with the
   pads where the main page has them, so you see (and can tap) the pad you edit. RESET in PITCH & SPEED puts its
   pitch, speed and reverse back. The SOUND page has the rest (half or double time, quality, chops, keyboard, pads).
   LAYER GROUP (PAD page, beside DRUMS) layers chops: put two or more pads in the same group (1-4) and a hit on any one
   of them plays them all together, each with its own settings and FX; letting go lets them all go. OFF: the pad alone.
10. FX page: an effect per pad. Pick the pad (tap it, or hit it), then a preset: LOFI, TELEPHONE, LOW PASS, HIGH PASS,
   BAND PASS, VINTAGE, LOW SHELF, HIGH SHELF, NOTCH, SWEEP (a filter sweeping up and down), RISER (a filter rising
   from the hit), ROBOT, RADIO, V CURVE, WARP-SPEED, TAPE (wow, flutter, a warm low end and a worn top; AMOUNT: how
   worn). Picking a preset turns the pad's FX on; the FX ON switch beside the list turns them off and back on. AMOUNT sets how much (on the shelves: the boost or cut), SWEEP / RISE how
   long a sweep or a rise takes (in time with your tempo), MIX dry to wet. COPY TO ALL PADS puts the pad's effect on
   every pad; CLEAR ALL FX takes them all off. Around the preset: DRIVE (saturation before it), TONE (darker or
   brighter after it), WIDTH (mono to wide), RESONANCE (the filter presets, SWEEP and RISER). Changes are heard at once,
   even on a pad that is playing. WARP-SPEED is a half-time effect: each SWEEP / RISE length (from the
   hit) plays its first half at half speed, an octave down, then catches up, so it stays in time; AMOUNT smooths the
   seams.

Q-Links
Each page has its own Q-Link set (NUDGE is Q-Link 2 on the PAD page). On the main page Q-Links 1-4 are ZOOM, SCROLL,
CUE and NUDGE, to find and place a chop by hand. MPC uses them when its Q-Link mode is SCREEN;
in PROJECT, PROGRAM or TRACK mode the Q-Links keep the main page's set on every page.

Your own sounds
BROWSE page: double-tap INTERNAL or SSD (or a USB drive), and the folders, to open them. Drag your finger up or down
on the list to scroll it; flick it and it coasts on, like MPC's own browser. The wheel moves the highlight a row a click,
the list following it a row at a time; its preview plays once the wheel stops.
Touch a row (or any button on the page) once, then turn the data wheel (MPC only sends the wheel to a plugin after a touch): each click highlights the next row and plays a preview of it (its first half
minute); its name shows at the top. Double-tap a row, or push the data wheel, to open the folder or load the WAV
(LOAD loads the highlighted one). BACK goes back a folder, to the page you were on, with the one you came from highlighted; DRIVES back to the
drives. The buttons leave the wheel on the list. A folder opens at its top. USER SOUNDS, at the top of the drives, has the WAVs in this folder (when there are any).
Once a sound came from a folder, the sound arrows at the top step through that folder's WAVs, and each one plays
from its start as it arrives. A pad hit or STOP ends a preview. A project remembers the sound by where it is.
Any bit depth and sample rate, mono or stereo, up to six minutes each.

Pads
The SOUND page shows the note each pad hit sends and which pad it played ("LAST: NOTE 72 > PAD A1"). If the pads
play the wrong chops, tap RESET PADS. If your pads send other notes, tap LEARN PADS and hit pads 1 to 16 in order;
tap it again to cancel (it also gives up after 20 seconds of no hits).
